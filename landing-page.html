The real question was never "how much did we spend."

Questions

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, and you cannot. Orium never asks for a bank login and never touches your accounts. You type in your balances and your schedules. That is deliberate: bank sync is unreliable here and blind to cash, and your money is not only in banks.

How much work is this every day?

Setup is the real effort, roughly one sitting. After that the recurring items run on their own. Day to day you are confirming that a bill was paid and logging the occasional grocery run.

My income is irregular. Does that break it?

No. Enter the income you can count on and leave the rest out. A forecast built on your reliable income is a conservative one, which is the useful direction to be wrong in. Windfalls only ever make the picture better.

How far ahead does it forecast?

Up to five years. Far enough to see a loan finish and a tuition year land, without pretending to know things nobody knows.

Can I try it before entering my real numbers?

Yes. New accounts can load a full sample family so you can click through a working forecast first, then wipe it and start on your own numbers whenever you are ready.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Orium works in the browser on phone, tablet and desktop, with every feature available on each.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Every record is tied to your account and locked to it at the database level. You can wipe everything or delete your account entirely from Settings at any time.
