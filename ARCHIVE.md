### Phase 11 — Budget replenish schedules + forecast deductions (scoped 2026-07-23, done)
Extends Budgets v3 (Phase 10) rather than replacing it: a budget still has a running ledger balance (`computeBudgetBalance`), but now gets a *schedule* driving when it replenishes, instead of only "connected to an income" (fires on settle, T56) or "manual" (Add/Take funds, T55). User feedback 2026-07-23: budgets should behave like a bill — a real, persisting future transaction the forecast can show ahead of time — not a value that only updates after the fact.

**The model:** a budget now has one of three replenish modes: **connected to an income** (unchanged from T56 — replenishes when that income settles), **replenish every** (new — the budget carries its own recurrence rule, the same day/week/month/year shape and `RecurrencePicker` UI as Bills/Income/Debt/Savings), or **manual** (unchanged from T55). Whichever schedule applies (the budget's own rule, or — new — its linked income's rule), every future occurrence of it now renders as its own Forecast row: a real deduction (−allocation) from cash flow, dated at the occurrence, exactly like a bill row — not a soft/uneditable projection like the old Phase 6B boundary rows. Settling that row (a bill-like settle action) does two things: records the cash-side settlement (a real outflow) and writes the budget's `'incoming'` ledger entry (the credit side, `computeBudgetBalance`'s input) — so the two stay in sync. An income-linked budget's row auto-settles when its linked income is settled (extending T56's existing hook), since that's the one moment the transfer actually happens; a "replenish every" budget's row has no other trigger, so the user settles it directly from the Forecast, same as a bill. A small new override table (`budget_replenish_overrides` — id/budget_id/original_date/skipped) marks a settled occurrence so it stops re-projecting, mirroring `occurrence_overrides` for recurring items, but without the move/edit fields (v1 scope: settle or leave projected, no mid-air date/amount edits before settling).

**Progress bar + "days until replenish":** for any budget with a resolvable schedule (own rule or linked income's rule), the Dashboard's Budgets card and the Budgets page show "X days until replenish" plus a time-based progress bar — fraction filled = elapsed time between the last scheduled occurrence and the next one, not spend-vs-allocation (there's no cycle/allocation target to measure spend against anymore, per Phase 10). Manual budgets have no resolvable schedule, so they show neither.
